FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How much does tree removal cost?

Every tree is different — height, lean, what's under it and how easy it is to reach. Send a few photos through the form and you'll get a ballpark first; the exact price is confirmed on site before any work starts.

What photos should I send?

One of the whole tree from a distance, one close to the trunk, and one showing what's nearby (house, fence, power lines). That's usually enough for a ballpark.

Which areas do you serve?

Crossville and nearby. Not sure about your street? Just call or text.
